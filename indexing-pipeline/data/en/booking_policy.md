# Booking and Stay Policy

## Check-in and Check-out Times
Check-in is from 2:00 PM onwards.
The latest check-in accepted is 10:00 PM. If you will arrive after 10:00 PM,
please call ahead on 081-598-1199.
Check-out is before 12:00 noon on the day of departure.

The resort does not accept check-in before 2:00 PM and does not offer luggage storage,
so guests arriving before 2:00 PM cannot leave their bags at the resort.
Late check-out: check-out after 12:00 noon is not possible.

## Required Documents
Guests must present: the original Thai ID card to staff at check-in; foreign guests show their passport instead.
Security deposit: the resort does not collect a damage deposit.

## Booking and Payment
Book through the resort's app, which is the main booking channel.
Call 081-598-1199 or message Facebook: Poonsuk Resort Sadao for enquiries and extra bed requests.
Guests pay the full room charge in the app in a single payment — there is no part-payment and no balance due at check-in —
by scanning the PromptPay QR code in the app and uploading the slip in the app within the time limit shown in the app.
If the slip is not uploaded in time, the booking is cancelled automatically and you can book again.
Staff check the slip, and the booking is confirmed once it has been checked.
The room charge is paid by bank transfer only — no cash and no credit cards. The extra bed fee, paid at the resort's front desk, can be paid in cash or by transfer.
The app accepts no more guests than the room allows. If you have one guest more than that, book in the app
for the number of guests the room allows, then call the owner on 081-598-1199 to request an extra bed
(at most one per room, 200 THB per bed per night).
The extra bed fee is paid at the resort on the day of check-in, not in the app.

## Cancellation Policy
You can cancel a booking yourself in the app, but only before the check-in date.
From the check-in date onwards it cannot be cancelled in the app — please contact the resort on 081-598-1199.
If the cancelled booking has already been paid, the system records a refund automatically,
and staff transfer the money back.
Full refund: cancelling before the check-in date gives a full refund; from the check-in date onwards there is no refund.
Refund timing: staff transfer the refund within 1 hour of cancellation.

## Date Changes
Changing your stay dates: you can change your stay dates by notifying the resort at least 2 days in advance.

## Pet Policy
The resort does not accept pets. Animals of any kind and any size, including dogs and cats,
are not permitted anywhere on the property.

## Smoking Policy
All rooms are non-smoking. Smoking inside the rooms is not permitted.
Designated smoking areas: there is an outdoor smoking area in the car park.
Penalty for violations: there is no special cleaning fee, but a fine applies if the resort's rules are not followed.

## Noise and Visitors
The resort's appeal is its quiet and privacy, so we ask guests not to disturb other rooms with noise.
Quiet hours: please keep noise down from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
In-room parties: parties are not permitted in the rooms.
Non-staying visitors: visitors who are not staying at the resort are not permitted on the property.
