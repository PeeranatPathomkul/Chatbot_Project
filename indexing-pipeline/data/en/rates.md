# Room Rates

## Room Rate
The nightly room rate is available from the booking system only. This document does
not state a figure, because rates change and a number left here would contradict
the rate a guest is actually charged.
The rate is the same every day — weekdays and weekends alike, with no weekend surcharge.
The rate is charged per room, not per person.

The rate does not include breakfast, as the resort does not serve breakfast.

## Extra Charges
Extra bed: 200 THB per bed per night, available in any room, charged on top of the
room rate.
The extra bed fee is charged per bed requested and per night, not per room.
How to work out a total: (nightly room rate from the booking system x rooms x nights)
+ (200 x extra beds x nights).

## Payment and Deposit
A booking is only confirmed once the room deposit has been paid.
Deposit: 200 THB.
Transfer to PromptPay number 081-599-3128.
Note: the PromptPay number 081-599-3128 differs from the contact number 081-598-1199,
but both belong to the resort. If unsure, call 081-598-1199 to confirm before transferring.
The balance is paid on the day of check-in.

The resort accepts bank transfer only — no cash and no credit cards.

## Not Included in the Rate
The room rate covers the room only. It does not include breakfast or an extra bed.
The resort has no breakfast service, no restaurant and no room service.
