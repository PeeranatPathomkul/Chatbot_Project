# Room Rates

## Room Rate
The nightly room rate is available from the booking system only. This document does
not state a figure, because rates change and a number left here would contradict
the rate a guest is actually charged.
The rate is the same every day — weekdays and weekends alike, with no weekend surcharge.
The rate is charged per room, not per person.

The rate does not include breakfast, as the resort does not serve breakfast.

## Extra Charges
Extra bed: 200 THB per bed per night, at most one extra bed per room.
The extra bed fee is charged per bed requested and per night, not per room.
The extra bed fee is not paid in the app — it is paid at the resort on the day of check-in.
The amount paid in the app is therefore the room charge only and does not include extra beds.
How to request an extra bed: book in the app for the number of guests the room allows,
then call the owner on 081-598-1199.

## Payment
Guests pay the full room charge in the app in a single payment, with no part-payment and no balance due at check-in.
How to pay
1. Book the room in the app. The booking is held awaiting payment.
2. Scan the PromptPay QR code in the app and transfer the full amount shown in the app.
3. Upload the transfer slip in the app within the time limit shown in the app.
4. Staff check the slip. Once it has been checked, the booking is confirmed.
If the slip is not uploaded within the time limit shown in the app, the booking is cancelled
automatically and you can book again.
The PromptPay number for the transfer is shown in the app at the payment step.

The resort accepts bank transfer only — no cash and no credit cards.

## Not Included in the Rate
The room rate covers the room only. It does not include breakfast or an extra bed.
The resort has no breakfast service, no restaurant and no room service.
