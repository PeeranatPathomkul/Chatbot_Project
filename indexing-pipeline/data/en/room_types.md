# Room Types

Each room at Poonsuk Resort is a separate small house.
There are 2 room types: twin-bed rooms (twin) and single-bed rooms (single).
Every room is the same size — 16 square metres (4 x 4 metres).
The number of rooms, room names, which room is which type, how many guests each room can be
booked for, in-room amenities and the nightly rate are all available from the booking system.
This document does not list them, because staff can change them in the booking system.

## Twin-Bed Rooms (twin)
These are the rooms whose type is twin in the booking system.
Each has two separate beds, ideal for friends or families who prefer separate beds.
Room size: 16 square metres (4 x 4 metres).
Can be booked in the app for the number of guests the room allows; one more guest can stay
if an extra bed is requested by phone.

## Single-Bed Rooms (single)
These are the rooms whose type is single in the booking system.
Each has one large bed, ideal for couples or two guests.
Room size: 16 square metres (4 x 4 metres).
Can be booked in the app for the number of guests the room allows; one more guest can stay
if an extra bed is requested by phone.

## All Rooms
The number of rooms, their names and which type each room is (twin or single) are available
from the booking system.
Every room is a detached house with its own private bathroom and parking close by.

## In-Room Amenities
In-room amenities differ from room to room. You can see each room's amenities in the app,
or ask the chatbot together with your stay dates. This document does not list them,
because staff can change them in the booking system.

## Extra Beds and Children
At most one extra bed can be added per room, for 200 THB per bed per night,
letting the room sleep one more guest.
The app has no extra bed option and accepts no more guests than the room allows.
How to request an extra bed: book in the app for the number of guests the room allows (for example, 2 guests),
then call the owner on 081-598-1199 to request the extra bed.
The extra bed fee is paid at the resort on the day of check-in and is not included in the room charge paid in the app.

The resort charges per room, not per person, so there is no separate child policy.
Children stay at no additional cost as long as the total number of guests stays within
the room's capacity. If you need an additional bed, the 200 THB per night extra bed fee applies,
paid at the resort on the day of check-in.
Baby cot: the resort has no baby cots to lend; please bring your own.

## Not Provided in the Rooms
Breakfast is not provided — the rate covers the room only.
There is no room service.
Smoking is not permitted inside the rooms.
Pets are not allowed.
