# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> Note for content editors: this file is meant to overlap with the other files.
> Guests ask in casual language, which matches an FAQ entry far more closely than a
> formally worded policy heading. Keep the "Q: ... / A: ..." format and leave a blank
> line between each pair. Keep this file consistent with data/th/faq.md.

Q: What time can I check in?
A: Check-in is from 2:00 PM onwards.

Q: What time is check-out?
A: Check-out is before 12:00 noon on your last day.

Q: How much does a room cost?
A: 650 THB per room per night. The rate is the same for every room type and every day of the week. If you book 3 or more rooms, or stay 3 or more nights, the rate drops to 600 THB per room per night.

Q: Is it more expensive on weekends?
A: No. The rate is 650 THB per night every day. There is no weekend or holiday surcharge.

Q: Do you have any promotions or discounts?
A: Yes. Book 3 or more rooms, or stay 3 or more consecutive nights, and the rate drops from 650 THB to 600 THB per room per night.

Q: What room types do you have?
A: Two types — single-bed rooms and twin-bed rooms. Both cost 650 THB per night and differ only in the number of beds. Both sleep 2 to 3 guests.

Q: How many people can stay in one room?
A: Each room sleeps 2 to 3 guests. A third guest needs an extra bed, which costs 200 THB per night.

Q: Can I request an extra bed? How much is it?
A: Yes, an extra bed can be added to any room for 200 THB per night. Please request it when you book.

Q: Do children stay free?
A: The resort charges per room rather than per person, so there is no separate child policy. Children stay at no extra cost as long as the total number of guests fits the room. If you need an additional bed, the 200 THB per night extra bed fee applies.

Q: Is breakfast included?
A: No. The resort does not serve breakfast and has no restaurant. The 650 THB rate covers the room only. Complimentary instant coffee sachets are provided in the room.

Q: Is there free Wi-Fi?
A: Yes, free Wi-Fi is available in every room at no extra charge.

Q: Is parking free?
A: Yes. Free, convenient and secure parking is available for guests, right next to the rooms.

Q: Can I get a refund if I cancel?
A: You may cancel at any time, but the deposit you have paid is non-refundable in all cases, no matter how far in advance you cancel.

Q: How do I book a room?
A: Call 081-598-1199 or message Facebook: Poonsuk Resort Sadao. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit has been paid.

Q: Can I bring my dog?
A: No. The resort does not accept pets of any kind or size, including dogs and cats.

Q: Can I bring my pet?
A: No. Pets are not permitted anywhere on the property under any circumstances.

Q: Can I smoke in the room?
A: No. All rooms are non-smoking and smoking inside the rooms is not permitted.

Q: Is there a swimming pool?
A: No. The resort is a small 6-room property focused on quiet and privacy, and has no pool.

Q: Is there a restaurant at the resort?
A: No. Guests can eat at the shops and markets nearby.

Q: Where is the resort located?
A: At 53/33 Moo 11, Liap Khlong Tha Phru Road, Sadao Sub-district, Sadao District, Songkhla Province — close to Khao Le, the market, Lotus's and the Sadao Halal Market.

Q: Does each room have a private bathroom?
A: Yes. Every room has its own clean, private bathroom inside the room.

Q: Do the rooms have air conditioning?
A: Yes, every room is air conditioned.

Q: What are the rooms like?
A: Each room is a Nordic-style detached small house. The rooms are clean and spacious, with air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom, and a table and chairs for relaxing inside the room.
